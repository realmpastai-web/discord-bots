### ✅ The Solution
A dedicated lead management bot that lives in your Discord server - where your team already works.

**Lead Management**
- Add leads instantly with `/lead-add`
- Track every lead through your pipeline
- Store contact info, addresses, and notes
- Never lose a lead again

**Follow-up System**
- Schedule follow-ups by type (call, email, text, visit)
- Daily follow-up reminders

**Pipeline Analytics**
- Visual dashboard of your sales funnel
- Track conversion rates
- See which lead sources perform best
- Know your numbers

**Lead Status Workflow**
